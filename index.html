About Me

A multi-disciplinary builder — code, design, and growth in one person.

I'm a Web Developer, Graphic Designer, App Developer, Digital Marketer and IT Expert based in Chandpur, Bangladesh. Over five years I've moved between the classroom and the command line — teaching by day, building websites, WordPress plugins, and brand assets by night.

I value family, faith, and integrity, and I bring the same discipline to a codebase as I do to a lesson plan: understand the problem fully, then build something clean and dependable. My long-term goal is to qualify as a BCS Cadre (Teacher) while continuing to grow Creative AI Institute and AR Agency alongside it.
